Methodology. Rows are compiled from exchange calendars and filings, regulator approvals (Saudi CMA), company disclosure and financial press reporting; each row carries its own source links and verification date, visible on expansion. Executive email addresses are almost never published, so none is presented as fact: addresses are marked verified only where the company publishes them, inferred where a confirmed domain pattern was applied to a named executive, and N/A otherwise. Inferred addresses are outreach hypotheses, not confirmed contacts. Where an exchange has a live pipeline but no named issuer, the exchange is retained as an explicit unnamed entry rather than dropped, because an empty exchange is itself information. Valuations are shown in their reported currency; the USD column used for sorting is an indicative normalisation only.
